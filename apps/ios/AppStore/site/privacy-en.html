Agent Hub Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Hefei Bocui Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. (合肥博璀人工智能科技有限公司, “we”) provides the Agent Hub iOS app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and its connection and notification services. Contact us at admin@51easyai.com for privacy or support requests.

Agent Hub connects to computers and agent services you select. Tasks execute on the selected computer. Messages, images and files you send may also be processed by AI services configured on that computer. Only connect to computers you own or are authorized to use, and check the target and model service before sending content.

1. Information and purposes

  • Connection and pairing: Computer names and addresses, host, project and conversation identifiers, device public keys, pairing keys and credentials identify the target, authorize connections and restore sessions. EasyAI sign-in also involves account identifiers, sign-in status and connection authorization. Credentials are stored in the system Keychain. The app does not ask for your computer’s Apple or Codex account password.
  • Messages and tasks: Messages, drafts, model responses, tool activity, approval answers and operation receipts display conversations, submit selected tasks, synchronize status and reconcile uncertain results. Sent content reaches the selected computer and its configured agent or model services.
  • Images and files: We process attachments you explicitly select, capture or upload, and files you request to download, for tasks, transfers and previews. The app does not automatically upload your entire photo library or file directories.
  • Voice input: You can choose phone system transcription or computer transcription. If the computer reports transcription unavailable, the app can use phone system transcription. The phone system uses on-device recognition when available or Apple's online speech service; this path does not send audio to your selected computer. After you allow sharing and end a computer recording, the original audio is sent to the selected computer, which uses its current Codex / ChatGPT login to call OpenAI transcription. Returned text remains an editable draft until you send it. Temporary audio supports transfer and recovery. Unsubmitted device audio is removed when cancelled; prepared audio is removed after successful insertion, and unfinished copies expire after 24 hours and are cleaned when accessed. Computer audio/transcripts are cleaned after their 24-hour expiry, while operation records remain to prevent duplicate submissions. Apple's or the selected transcription provider's policies govern their processing.
  • Notifications: When enabled, app-generated device identifiers, APNs push tokens, target and conversation identifiers, subscription preferences, events and delivery receipts support reminders, revocation and notification recovery. The implemented encrypted notification channel sends encrypted summaries to its relay. The relay still handles routing identifiers, push tokens, timestamps and delivery status.
  • Local state and diagnostics: Settings, drafts, recently read conversation caches, unread state, unresolved operations and downloaded files are stored on the device for use and recovery. Connection or notification services may process necessary error and operational logs. Servers hosting our website or services may record IP addresses, access times, requested paths and browser information for security and troubleshooting.

Optional Task Hub: A separately configured service processes member identifiers, device sessions, project permissions, tasks, feedback, artifacts, acceptance materials and operation receipts. Your service operator controls that service and its member accounts, retention and deletion. Direct LAN Agent access does not require a Task Hub account.

2. Permissions

The camera scans pairing codes; the microphone records voice input and speech recognition enables phone system transcription; local network permission discovers and connects to selected computers; notifications provide task reminders. Permissions are requested when needed. You can withdraw them in iOS Settings; affected features stop or become unavailable, while features that do not need those permissions remain usable. System pickers control image and file selection.

3. Recipients and third parties

Before the first task, attachment or continuation is submitted to a real computer and its model, the app identifies the computer, agent and model and requests explicit data-sharing permission. You may decline without sending the draft. Allowing permission requires you to submit again; it never replays captured input. Permission is stored on this device and can be revoked in Settings > AI data sharing. The permission scope is shown in the app. Following computer configuration permits that target's current and subsequent AI configuration; check its actual provider and processing policy on the computer. A different Agent or machine/runtime target requires confirmation again. The local UI example does not send content and does not need this permission.

Your selected computer and agent services receive your tasks and attachments. Services used by Codex may include OpenAI; EasyWork and other connections use the model or service providers actually configured for them. Their retention, training use and processing locations depend on their policies and your account settings.

Apple provides system permissions, file and photo selection and APNs, subject also to Apple’s Privacy Policy. Enabled connection and notification services and their infrastructure providers handle data necessary to operate those services. Direct computer task content is not automatically uploaded to our business servers solely because you use this app. Other relay or model connections follow the actual selected configuration.

If you contact support, email systems process your email address and the information and attachments you send. Do not include passwords, complete access keys or unrelated private conversations. We do not sell personal information or use app data for cross-app advertising tracking. The app does not integrate advertising tracking SDKs. We may disclose necessary information to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, or protect service security.

4. Retention and deletion

Local settings, drafts, caches and downloaded files are retained for use and recovery. Removing a client stops that connection; it does not delete conversations on the computer or necessarily erase all cached history immediately. Deleting the app removes its app-container data. Separately exported files, system backups and Keychain items that iOS may retain must be managed separately. Revoking a computer-side key invalidates old credentials.

Content already sent to computers, model providers or other services is retained under their configuration and policies and must be deleted there. The notification relay cleans up completed delivery records according to expiry; the current implementation normally cleans them up seven days after expiry. Subscriptions, device bindings and necessary revocation state may remain longer to maintain delivery and prevent old requests from restoring a subscription. Turning off notifications or removing a client requests subscription revocation, which may require connectivity to complete.

Services we control retain information only for service operation, security troubleshooting and applicable legal requirements. You can ask us to check or delete information we control. We may verify your identity and explain retention required by law, others’ rights or technical limitations.

5. Security and processing locations

We use the system Keychain for credentials and encryption for applicable caches, notification summaries and migration files. Connection security depends on the address and transport you select. Prefer trusted HTTPS or paired secure connections. Local HTTP connections, exported files, self-hosted services and your computers also need your protection. Notification metadata and all task traffic do not all share a single end-to-end encrypted channel.

Data may be processed in your region, where your computer is located, or where Apple or your actual model or relay provider operates. Check the selected service’s data policies before sending. We cannot promise a fixed processing region or retention period for third parties.

6. Your choices and rights

Revoking AI data-sharing permission blocks future submissions. It does not automatically delete content already delivered or stop a running task. Stop tasks or request deletion at the relevant computer/service. When following the computer's configuration, permission covers the services configured there; check the actual provider on the computer. The app cannot read or promise every third-party provider's policies.

You can modify or remove connections, disable notifications, withdraw system permissions, edit unsent drafts and manage sent content on the selected computer or third-party service. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent or object to processing. Email us with the service and scope of your request, without complete access keys. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

7. Children and updates

Agent Hub serves users who manage computer tasks and is not directed at children. We do not actively request children’s personal information. A guardian who believes a child’s information has been handled improperly can contact us. If features, recipients or practices change, we update this policy and its date and provide notices or seek renewed permission where required.
