# EasyWork authorization residue as immutable archives

This candidate extends the framed Android migration with a versioned, lossless audit graph for current EasyWork authorization residue and unreferenced credential candidates. It does not import active EasyWork clients. The existing active EasyWork metadata/business-graph guard remains; mixed active-client descriptors, caches and immutable business operations still need their separate projection before full P7 can complete.

The inventory covers easywork-device-identity, every authenticated easywork-credential name including unreferenced P8 per-action candidates, device grants, authorization fences, pairing claims and OIDC last/account/pending records. A fence remains an original even when its grant is gone, including a guard written before the grant and pending/confirmed revoke with a saved original. Modern authenticated names reveal exact legacy grant/fence aliases through original references; unidentifiable hash-only legacy records reject the whole inventory. No guessed alias or best-effort omission is permitted.

migration-easywork-archives-v1 contains a bounded closed installation catalog. Each row binds public original context, requiresOriginalInstallation=true and exact original name/role/size/SHA-256 descriptors. Raw originals move to migration-easywork-original-v1 aliases under the archive ID. Sixteen installations,1024 originals per installation,512KiB catalog, the existing8MiB text-record bound,1024 output records and192MiB combined raw budget remain explicit limits. Quota/corruption/future major protocol failures reject the whole candidate.

The frozen reader verifies original X25519 public/private correspondence, binding/target/account relationships, grant/ref relationships, claim public JWK/thumbprint and exact known operation/body/hash relations. Access/refresh/pending tokens remain opaque43-character ASCII originals; they are not decoded or normalized as keys. Grant and offer IDs remain bounded strings, as in the primary EWDAP schema. Original accepted UUID operation spellings stay exact; fence epoch retains its producer's canonical rule. Required protocol/version/type invariants are checked while root/grant/pending/audit extensions already preserved by P8 remain exact. Expired historical OIDC/session/claim facts remain readable without exchanging or refreshing anything. This is an audit parser, not proof of a live login or valid current authorization.

No reader calls EasyWorkIdentity.load, DPoP load/proof, a coordinator, session/channel, token refresh, revoke, pairing or HTTP. System DPoP private keys/aliases are not exported. Original X25519 identity/credential/grant/PKCE/operation text remains only inside password-encrypted inert aliases; it never becomes incoming active metadata, a credentialRef mapping, OIDC callback state or current authority. Destination-local original authority remains exact under its own installation, and its prior archives merge without borrowing another archive's missing material. The existing exact-root/candidate/quiescence lease is also required for this family. Unknown source operations never replay.
